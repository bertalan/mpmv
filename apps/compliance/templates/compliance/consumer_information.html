{% translate "Right of withdrawal" %}

{% blocktranslate with days=info.withdrawal.period_days %}You may withdraw from a distance contract within {{ days }} days without giving a reason. The period runs from the day you receive the goods. Each seller in your order is a separate contract.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "To withdraw online, use the button below at any time during the withdrawal period. You enter the order number and your email address, choose the parts to withdraw from, and confirm. You receive an acknowledgement by email with the content, the date and the time." %}

{% translate "Effects of withdrawal" %}

{% translate "The seller refunds every payment received for the contract, including the standard delivery costs, without undue delay and at the latest 14 days after learning of your withdrawal. You return the goods without undue delay and at the latest 14 days after your withdrawal. You are liable for any loss of value caused by handling beyond what is needed to check the goods." %}

{% translate "Exclusions and early expiry" %}

{% translate "There is no right of withdrawal for goods made to your specifications or clearly personalised, for goods that spoil quickly, and for sealed goods that are not suitable for return for health or hygiene reasons once you have unsealed them.
