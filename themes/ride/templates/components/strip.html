{% load i18n theme_tags %}{% comment %} Multi-item strip of the `ride` theme (THM.14): products, vendors, testimonials or blog posts in a Bootstrap carousel. The items are grouped by the `in_groups` filter, one group per slide, so a slide shows up to `per_view` items side by side (fewer columns on a narrow screen: the group wraps). Previous, Pause/Play and Next are buttons, the indicators are labelled buttons, the arrow keys work once the focus is inside. Nothing moves unless `autoplay` is set; then it pauses on hover and focus and never runs under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Without JavaScript all items are listed one after the other. {% include "components/strip.html" with id="featured" label=_("Featured products") slides=products per_view=4 only %} Context: - `id` (required): unique on the page; `label` (required): the carousel's accessible name. - `slides`: a list of `title`, optional `text`, `meta`, `url`, `image_url` and `image_alt` (a testimonial: `text` is the quote, `title` the author). - `per_view`: items per slide, 1 to 4 (default 4); `heading_level`: of the item titles (default 3). - `autoplay` (default off) and `interval` in ms (default 6000). {% endcomment %}{% firstof per_view 4 as size %}{% with groups=slides|in_groups:size %}{% with total=groups|length %}
