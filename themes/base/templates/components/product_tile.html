{% load i18n %}{% comment %} Product tile (THM.4, spec 14 §14.9): one product in a grid or a list. The product name is the tile's only link and its `::after` covers the whole tile, so a click on the image follows it and screen readers hear one link named by the product (no nested interactive elements). Prices arrive formatted by the caller (catalog services); the tile only shows them. {% include "components/product_tile.html" with name=p.name url=p.url price=p.price_display vendor=p.vendor_name only %} Context: - `name` (required), `url`: the product name and its page; without `url` (a product with no page yet) the name shows without a link. - `heading_level`: 2 to 6 (default 3), to fit the page's outline. - `image_url`, `image_alt`: the product image; `image_alt` describes what the image adds, or is empty when it only repeats the name. `image_width` and `image_height` (default 400 x 400) reserve its space. Without `image_url` a neutral placeholder, hidden from assistive technology, keeps the grid aligned. - `price`: the formatted price; empty means "Price on request". - `price_before`: the previous price, struck through and announced as "Was" (a sale). - `vendor`: the vendor's name, shown as "Sold by …". - `badges`: a list of badge kinds (components/badge.html): sale, new, out_of_stock, on_request. "out_of_stock" also mutes the tile. {% endcomment %}
