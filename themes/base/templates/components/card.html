{% comment %} Card (THM.4, spec 14 §14.9): a titled box for an article teaser, a service or a landing block. With a `url`, the title link is the card's only interactive element and its `::after` covers the whole card, so a click anywhere follows it and screen readers hear one link named by the title (no nested links or buttons, WCAG 2.4.4). {% include "components/card.html" with title=page.title url=page.url text=page.search_description only %} Context: - `title` (required): the heading text, and the link's accessible name. - `heading_level`: 2 to 6 (default 3), to fit the page's outline. - `url`: makes the whole card a link. - `text`: one short paragraph under the title. - `meta`: a muted line under the title, such as a date or a category. - `image_url`, `image_alt`: an image on top; `image_alt` describes it, or stays empty when the image only decorates (the title already names the card). `image_width` and `image_height` (default 640 x 400) reserve its space. {% endcomment %}
