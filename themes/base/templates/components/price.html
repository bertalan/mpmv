{% load i18n %}{% comment %} Price (THM.4, spec 14 §14.9): the price of a product page, formatted by the caller (catalog services). A reduction has one reference price only, the lowest price of the last 30 days before it (Omnibus, Directive 2019/2161; spec 11, CMP.6): it is struck through and labelled "Lowest price in the last 30 days". There is deliberately no input for any other previous price (a list price, the price before the last change): a second reference is the pattern consumer authorities sanction, so a `before` passed by mistake is ignored. {% include "components/price.html" with amount=p.price lowest_30_days=p.lowest_30_days only %} Context: - `amount` (required unless `on_request`): the current price, formatted. - `lowest_30_days`: only when the product is on a price reduction, the lowest price it had in the 30 days before the reduction, formatted (from the catalog's price history). The price is then shown as reduced, with this value struck through as its only reference. Leave it out when there is no reduction; the caller decides, because the amounts are formatted strings. - `note`: a line under the price, such as "VAT included" or "Shipping calculated at checkout". - `from_price`: the amount is the lowest of several variants ("From € 39.00"). - `on_request`: no amount; "Price on request". {% endcomment %}
