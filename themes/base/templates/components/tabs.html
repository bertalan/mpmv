{% comment %} Tabs (THM.4, spec 14 §14.9), only where the content is short, such as a product's description, size guide and care notes. Progressive enhancement: without JavaScript every panel is shown under its own heading and the tab list stays `hidden`. The `tabs` Alpine component (themes/base/js/base.js, CSP build) shows the list, hides the headings and all panels but the selected one, and gives the panels `role="tabpanel"`. Keyboard (WAI-ARIA tabs pattern): one tab stop for the list (roving tabindex); Left and Right arrows move between the tabs and wrap, Home and End go to the first and last; selection follows focus; Tab moves on into the open panel. {% include "components/tabs.html" with id="product-info" label=_("Product details") tabs=sections only %} Context: - `id` (required): a prefix for the ids of the tabs and panels, unique on the page. - `label` (required): the accessible name of the tab list. - `tabs`: a list of `label` and `content`; the content is escaped unless the caller marks it safe (rich text rendered by the CMS, for example). - `selected`: the tab selected on load, 1-based (default 1). - `heading_level`: the level of the no-script panel headings, 2 to 6 (default 3). {% endcomment %}
