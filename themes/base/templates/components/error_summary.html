{% load i18n %}{% comment %} Error summary (THM.4, spec 14 §14.6): at the top of a form that failed validation, a titled box listing every error. A field error links to its control (the first option of a radio or checkbox group); an error of the whole form, or of a hidden field, is plain text (WCAG 3.3.1, 3.3.3). Nothing renders for an unbound or valid form.
