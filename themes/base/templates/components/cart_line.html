{% load i18n theme_tags %}{% comment %} Cart line (THM.4, spec 14 §14.9): one product in the cart, as a list item of a per-vendor package (ORD.5). The product name is a link; the quantity stepper and the remove button belong to the cart form that wraps the list (the remove button submits `remove=`). Prices arrive formatted by the caller. A problem (stock dropped, product withdrawn) is said in words next to the line, never by colour alone.
