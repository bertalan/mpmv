# Categories expected to declare fitment
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This page lists every category and says whether its items are expected to declare what they fit. At first none is expected.

## What you can do
- Narrow the list with the filter Fitment (Expected or Not expected) and Filter.
- Tick categories, choose an action under Action on the selected rows, either Expect fitment or Do not expect fitment, then Preview.
- The preview page lists the changes. Nothing is written until you confirm.
- The column Declaration required to publish shows whether a category has the gate on. It is off by default. To turn it on, choose Require a declaration to publish; to turn it off, choose Do not require a declaration to publish. Turning it on also marks the category as expected to declare fitment.

## Good to know
With the gate on, a product for sale in that category cannot be published until it says which items it fits, or that it fits everything. Catalogue-only products are not affected. The gate is not retroactive: products that are already published stay online, and the rule applies the next time someone publishes a product.

Only a role with the write right sees the actions. Read-only roles can use the list and filter.
