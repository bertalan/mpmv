# Terminations
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This page lists the notices of termination given to vendors and, for those who may write, the form to give one.

## What you see
- A table with Vendor, Notice given, The service ends on and Status. The vendor name opens the notice. Narrow the list with Status and Filter.
- The text above the table states the minimum notice period in force. The vendor keeps selling and can download its data until the end date.

## Give notice
Give notice of termination appears only for staff who may write. Complete the form and choose Give notice and tell the vendor: the notice is recorded and the vendor is told. Giving notice may ask for your passkey.

## Good to know
A vendor with a pending notice cannot receive a second one. A shorter period needs one of the exceptions in the list.
