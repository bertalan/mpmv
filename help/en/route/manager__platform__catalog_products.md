# Products
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This page lists the products of every shop, for the Catalogue and moderation role. The platform owner and the Support role can read it but cannot change anything: for them the page shows a notice and no change controls, and any attempt to change a product is refused (access denied).

## Find products
- **Search** looks at the SKU and at the titles in every language.
- **Shop**, **Status**, **Category** and **Translations** narrow the list. Under **Translations**, **Complete** means every language is published and up to date; **Outdated** means a text was made from an older source text.
- The **Translations** column shows the state of each language: missing, draft, reviewed, published or outdated.

## Change several products
1. Tick the rows.
2. Choose **Publish**, **Unpublish (back to draft)** or **Retire (page live, not purchasable)** and select **Preview**.
3. Check the changes and select **Confirm**. Nothing changes before you confirm.

Publishing is refused for the whole selection when one product does not meet the publication rules; the message says why. Every change is recorded in the audit log. Open a SKU to see the product.
