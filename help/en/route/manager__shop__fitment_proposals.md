# Propose a missing item
last-checked: 2026-11-18

Use this page when a make, model or variant is not in the lists.

## What you can do
- Type the **Name of the missing item**, choose where it sits under **Belongs under**, then select **Send the proposal**.
- **Your proposals** shows each proposal and its status.

## Good to know
A proposal stays hidden from buyers and from the pickers until the platform approves it. A shop can have at most 20 proposals waiting; the next one is refused until some are reviewed.
