# Order
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This page shows one order of your shop and the next step you can take. It shows the customer's name and, while the order is open, the shipping address. It does not show the customer's e-mail or billing data.

## What you can do
- **Accept the order** when it is new.
- **Ship the order**: enter the **Tracking number** and the **Carrier**, then press **Mark as shipped**.
- **Mark as delivered** once the goods have arrived.
- **Change this order** opens the page to correct a paid order that is not shipped yet.
- Under **Invoice**, press **I have issued the invoice or receipt** after you have issued your own invoice or receipt. The platform does not issue it for you.

Only the action that is allowed at the current **Status** is shown. If you change an order after you issued an invoice, a reminder asks you to correct the document.

Use **Back to the orders** to return to the list.
