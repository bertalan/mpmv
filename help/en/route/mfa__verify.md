# Verify it is you
last-checked: 2026-11-17

## What this page is for

After your password, this page asks for a second proof that it is you.

## What you can do

- Use your passkey, or enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app and press Verify.
- If you lost your device, enter one of your recovery codes.

## Good to know

- Each recovery code works once.
- An error message appears above the form and points to the field you used.
