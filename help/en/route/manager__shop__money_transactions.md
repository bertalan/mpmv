# Transactions
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This page lists every movement of your shop's balance, newest first.

## What you see
- **Type** says what the movement is, for example a sale, a commission or a payout.
- **Amount (EUR)** has a plus sign when money is credited to your shop and a minus sign when it is debited.
- **Balance (EUR)** is your balance right after that movement.
- **Order** links to the order the movement belongs to, and **Reference** shows the payment provider's reference when there is one.

## What to do
Choose a period with **From** and **To**, and a **Type** if you want only one kind of movement, then press **Filter**. Press **Export as CSV** to download the movements of that period (the current month if you set no period). The file is a non-fiscal document: it carries a reference starting with LX- that you can quote to your accountant or to us.
