# Statement
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This page sums up your shop's money for a period, the current month by default. It is a non-fiscal document, not an invoice: it has a reference starting with LX- that stays the same for the same period.

## What you see
- **Opening balance** is your balance when the period starts.
- **Credits** and **Debits** are the money credited to and debited from your shop in the period.
- **Closing balance** is your balance when the period ends.
- **Totals by type** splits the period by kind of movement.

## What to do
Choose a period with **From** and **To** and press **Filter**. A statement needs both dates: with only one, the current month is shown.
