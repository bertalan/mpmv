# Cancel the event
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This page asks you to confirm that you cancel an event of your shop. Nothing is cancelled until you confirm.

## What happens
- The event stays online with a message that it is cancelled, in every language.
- The cancellation cannot be undone.

## What to do
- Press **Cancel the event** to confirm.
- Press **Keep the event** if you changed your mind: the event stays as it is.

Only an event that is online can be cancelled. For an event that is still a draft, you do not need to cancel it: just do not submit it.
