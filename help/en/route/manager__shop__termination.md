# Termination notice
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This page shows whether the platform has given your shop a notice of termination. If there is none, it says so.

## If there is a notice
- **Notice given** and **The service ends on** are the two dates. Until the end date you can keep selling and download your data.
- **Shorter notice because** appears only when a shorter notice applies.
- **Facts and circumstances** and **Ground** explain the decision.

The page also tells you that you can challenge the decision. How is described in the platform terms.

## Your data
Press **Download my data** to get your shop profile, products and order amounts as a JSON file. The download is available only while a notice is pending, and too many downloads in a row are refused for a while: try again later.
