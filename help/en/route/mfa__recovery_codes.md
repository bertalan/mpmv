# Recovery codes
last-checked: 2026-11-17

## What this page is for

Recovery codes let you sign in if you lose your authenticator app or passkey. This page shows how many you have left.

## What you can do

- Press Generate new codes to get a fresh set.
- Store the new codes somewhere safe right away.

## Good to know

- New codes make every previous code stop working.
- The codes are shown only once, when they are generated.
- Use Back to two-factor settings to return.
