# Fitment scheme
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This page defines what customers pick when they say what an item fits: for example a make and a model, or a brand and a machine. It has settings and a list of levels, at most four.

## How changes work
Nothing is written at once. Each change first shows a preview, and you confirm on the preview page. Writing needs the right role and may ask for your passkey. Without the writing right you can only read the scheme.

## What you can do
- Edit the fields under Settings and choose Preview the change.
- Under Levels, open a level to rename its labels (Preview the labels), use Switch names by language or Remove this level.
- Give new positions under Order (Preview the order) or Add a level (Preview the level).

## Good to know
A level that already has nodes cannot be reordered, removed or changed in how it names things.
