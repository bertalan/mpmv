# My garage
last-checked: 2026-11-20

## What this page is for

This page lists the vehicles you saved. The one marked active decides what the shop shows you as fitting.

## What you can do

- Make a vehicle active, or reset to show everything again. Reset keeps your vehicles.
- Rename or duplicate a vehicle.
- Delete a vehicle. You are asked to confirm first, and a deleted vehicle cannot be brought back.

## Good to know

- You can keep a limited number of vehicles. When the garage is full, delete one to make room.
- A vehicle marked "no longer in the catalogue" still works as before. Delete it and save a new one in its place.
