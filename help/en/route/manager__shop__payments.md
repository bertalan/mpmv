# Payments
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This page is for the owner of the shop. Here you connect the account that receives the money from your sales (the payout account).

## What you see
- **Account status** tells you whether the account is connected and active.
- **Selling mode** is **Online sale** or **On request**.
- **Still needed** lists what the payment provider still asks of you, when something is missing.

Until the account is active, your products are shown in the catalogue and are sold on request: customers cannot add them to the cart.

## What to do
Press **Connect your payout account** to start, or **Continue the onboarding** if you started before. You are taken to the payment provider to complete the steps. When you come back, the status is updated.

Do not close the provider's steps halfway: the account stays inactive until everything asked is done.
