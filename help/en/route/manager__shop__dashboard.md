# Dashboard
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This is the first page of your shop area. It shows which shop you are working on and what you are allowed to do in it. The page is read only.

## What you see
- **Vendor** is the shop you are working on. **Your role** is **Owner** or **Staff**.
- Under **Products** you see four counts: **Published**, **Drafts**, **Retired** and **Out of stock**.

## Good to know
- The menu shows only the pages your role may use. Some pages, such as the storefront profile, the sales terms and the payments, are for owners only.
- If you belong to several shops, check the shop name before you change anything.
- If a count looks wrong, first make sure you are working on the right shop. Questions: {{ operator.support_email }}.
