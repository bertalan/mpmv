# Staff and roles
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This page shows who holds which platform role and lets the platform owner give or revoke a role. Vendor members cannot hold a platform role.

## What you see
The table Platform staff lists each Account with its Roles. If the one-person-team setting is on, a warning stays on screen: one person may then hold incompatible roles.

## Give or revoke a role
- Under Give a role, fill in the e-mail of the account, the role and a reason, then choose Give the role.
- A role is removed with the button to revoke next to it.

## Safeguards
Changes need operational mode and are recorded in the audit log. Granting the owner role also asks for a fresh passkey.

## Good to know
Check the e-mail before you confirm: a role takes effect for that account.
