# Proposed nodes
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This is the review queue of nodes proposed by sellers or staff. A proposed node stays invisible to pickers until it is approved.

## What you can do
- Tick proposals, choose Approve under Action on the selected rows, then Preview. Nothing is written until you confirm.
- Open a proposal by its name to refuse it, or to approve it after a rename.
- All nodes and New node are in the links above the table.

## Good to know
A child proposed under a proposed parent is approved after its parent. Only a role with the write right sees the action.
