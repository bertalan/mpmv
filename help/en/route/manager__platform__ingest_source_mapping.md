# Map the columns
last-checked: 2026-11-28

Upload a sample to see its column names, then choose for each column the field it fills. The SKU and the title are required; a column you do not need is ignored. The sample file is not kept. Saving the mapping makes an earlier sample out of date: read the sample again on the checklist.

The mapping belongs to the vendor, not to one source: it applies to all of this vendor's sources, and saving it can make their earlier samples out of date.
