# Sales terms
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This page is for the owner of the shop. The sales terms are the conditions buyers read before they pay.

## What you see
- The version in force and its date, with **Open the public page**. If none is published yet, your products are sold on request only.
- **Standard clause on applicable law and court**: a model text you can read.
- **Versions**: every version published so far.

## Publish a new version
Fill in the form (for example **Sales terms**, **Governing law**, **Delivery time in days**, **Who pays the return**) and press **Publish**. The new version goes into force at once. A published version is never edited. Orders already placed keep the version they were placed under.

Read the form carefully before you publish: you cannot change the version afterwards, only publish a newer one. Questions: {{ operator.support_email }}.
