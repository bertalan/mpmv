# Order received
last-checked: 2026-11-17

## What this page is for

The order is placed. You see your order number, the total and the payment step.

## What you can do

- Pay with the form on the page and press Pay now.
- Keep the order number and the confirmation e-mail.

## Good to know

- After you pay, the page shows that the payment is being confirmed and updates by itself. Do not pay again.
- If the payment form does not load, reload the page after a moment.
- If online payment is not open yet, the items stay held for you for a short time.
