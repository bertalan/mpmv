# Images
last-checked: 2026-11-17

This is the image library. Every picture used on the site comes from here, and each one needs its licence details.

## What you can do
- Search or filter the library, or add images with the upload button.
- Open an image to set its title, its focal point and its licence, author, source address, credit text and permission reference.
- If your role shows it, open Images to classify in the menu to find pictures that still lack a licence or an author.

## Good to know
- Only licences from the allow list can be chosen: they allow commercial use and adaptation. Non-commercial and no-derivatives licences are not accepted.
- The licence says what else is required, such as a source address, a permission reference or a visible credit. The save is refused while it is missing.
- The alt text, which describes the picture for people who cannot see it, is written where the picture is used.
