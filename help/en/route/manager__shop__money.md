# Money
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This page is for the owner of the shop. It shows the money of your shop as the platform records it: what you are owed, what was paid to you and every movement in between. Nothing on these pages changes your balance.

## What you see
- **Current balance** is what the platform owes your shop right now. A negative balance means your shop owes the platform: it is set off against your next sales.
- **Latest payouts** lists the last amounts sent to your payout account.
- **Latest transactions** lists the last movements: sales, commissions, fees, refunds and payouts.

## What to do
Use the tabs **Transactions**, **Payouts** and **Statement** to see more, choose a period or download your movements.
