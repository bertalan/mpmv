# Two-factor authentication
last-checked: 2026-11-17

## What this page is for

This page lists the ways you confirm it is you after your password, and lets you add or remove them.

## What you can do

- Add an authenticator app or a passkey.
- Remove a factor you no longer use.
- Open Manage recovery codes to see how many are left.

## Good to know

- Keep at least one working factor and your recovery codes, so you cannot be locked out.
- Some roles must have a second factor before they can continue.
