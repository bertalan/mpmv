# Edit the event
last-checked: 2026-11-18

Use this page to change an event of your shop and to prepare it for review.

## What you can do
- Change the fields and press **Save draft**. Every save is a draft.
- Open **Preview the saved draft** to see how the page will look.
- Under **Content**, add, edit, move or remove the blocks of the event page. Each change is saved as a draft.
- Under **Submit**, press **Submit for review** to send the saved draft to the platform moderators.
- **Cancel the event** appears for an event that is online.

If the moderators asked for changes or hid the event, a message at the top of the page tells you why.

Remember: submit only after you saved. A translation takes dates, place and products from the original event. Pictures come from your image library, linked under the form.
