# Fitment report
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This report shows where your fitment data needs work.

## What you can see
- **Products still undeclared**: products in a category that needs fitment with nothing declared.
- **Rows waiting on an item that is not approved**: products with a row on an item that is proposed or retired.
- **Exclusions with nothing to exclude from**: an exclusion without a row that fits above it.

## Good to know
Select a SKU to open its **Fits** page and fix it.
