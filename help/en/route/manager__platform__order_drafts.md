# Prepared carts
last-checked: 2026-11-18

Use this page to see the carts you prepared for customers from one of their orders, while they wait for the customer to open the link.

## Prepare a cart
1. Open the order in Order changes: type its number and open one of its packages.
2. Under Prepare a cart for the customer, write the note (who asked and how) and choose Prepare the cart.

The whole order is copied for the same customer at today's prices and stock. It is not an order: nothing is reserved or charged.

## The list
Each row shows the order number, whether the customer has an account or ordered as a guest, when the cart was prepared, the number of lines and whether a usable link was sent. Choose an order number to open the cart.

A cart leaves the list once the customer opens its link.
