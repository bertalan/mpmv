# AI provider
last-checked: 2026-11-28

This page is where the shop owner chooses the service that writes the texts of your own products, and gives it your own key.

## What you set
- **AI provider** is the service to use.
- **Model** is the model name as the provider writes it.
- **API key** is your own key for that provider. It is stored encrypted and never shown again. Leave it empty to keep the key you saved before. If you change the provider, enter the key for the new one: the old key is deleted.
- **Remove the saved key** deletes the key. Without a key, no AI writes your texts.

## Good to know
- Only the shop owner can save this page, and saving it asks you to confirm with your passkey.
- The service receives product data only, never customer data. Every text waits for your review before it is shown.
- Without a saved key, no AI writes your texts.
