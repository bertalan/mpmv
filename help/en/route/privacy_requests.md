# Erasure requests
last-checked: 2026-11-17

## What this page is for

This page is for staff. It lists customers' account erasure requests and their state.

## What you can do

- Check the request reference, the date and whether it is pending or completed.
- For a pending request, choose a reason and press Process now.

## Good to know

- Each request is processed one at a time and cannot be undone.
- If you do not see this page, your account may not have the staff role.
