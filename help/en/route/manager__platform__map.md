# Platform map
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This page lists every area of the site, grouped, so that staff can find a screen quickly.

## How to read it
- An entry with a link opens that page.
- Shop space pages are shown as text with their address: staff do not open them directly.

## Good to know
The page only lists and changes nothing. A linked page may still refuse you if your role does not include it.
