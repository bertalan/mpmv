# Choose a vendor
last-checked: 2026-11-18

You see this page because you are a member of several shops. Choose the one you want to work on.

## How to use it
- Press the name of the shop. The next pages show that shop only: its orders, products and settings.
- You can switch shop later from the header.

## Good to know
- Your role can differ from shop to shop. The dashboard shows your role in the shop you chose.
- Before you change something, check the shop name in the page: it is easy to work on the wrong shop when you manage several.

If a shop is missing from the list, write to {{ operator.support_email }}.
