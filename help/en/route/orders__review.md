# Review and pay
last-checked: 2026-11-17

## What this page is for

This is the last check before you place the order. You see the delivery details, each seller's package, the shipping choice and the total.

## What you can do

- Choose a shipping option for each seller; the total updates.
- Use Change details if the address is wrong.
- Tick the terms box and press the button to place the order.

## Good to know

- Placing the order holds the items for you. You pay in the next step.
- If the button is missing, solve the points shown above it first.
