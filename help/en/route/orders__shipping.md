# Shipping choice
last-checked: 2026-11-17

## What this page is for

You do not open this address yourself. It saves the shipping option you pick on the review page and shows the updated total.

## What you can do

- Pick a shipping option for each seller on the review page; the choice is saved straight away.
- Without JavaScript, press Update shipping.

## Good to know

- If your cart or address is missing, you are sent back to the cart.
- Too many changes in a short time make the page ask you to wait a moment.
