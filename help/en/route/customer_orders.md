# My orders
last-checked: 2026-11-17

## What this page is for

This list shows your orders, newest first, with date, status and total.

## What you can do

- Open an order by its number to see the details.
- Use the page links at the bottom to move through older orders.

## Good to know

- If the list is empty, you have not placed an order yet.
- The status is the one we have for the order now.
