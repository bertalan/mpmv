# Images
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This page is the image library of your shop. You can use these pictures in the event pages.

## What you see
Under **Your images**, each picture with its title, author and licence. If there are none, the page says "You have no images yet."

## Add a picture
Under **Upload an image**, choose the **File**. Every picture needs a **Licence** and an **Author**; give a **Source address** or a **Permission reference** when the licence requires it. Then press **Upload**. The picture is saved in your library.

Check that you have the right to use a picture before you upload it. The author and the licence are shown with the picture.

Use **Back to the events** to return to the list.
