# Change an order
last-checked: 2026-11-18

Use this page to change a paid order that is waiting for you or already accepted, and not yet shipped. After that, the page tells you that the order can no longer be changed.

## How it works
1. Under **Prepare a change**, choose what to change, for example a quantity or the **Shipping method**, and write the **Reason for the change**. The customer reads this reason, so use plain words.
2. Press **Compute the change**.
3. Under **The change being prepared**, check **What changes**, the totals and **Money**: whether the customer is refunded or has to pay.
4. Press **Apply the change**, or **Discard the draft**.

If applying the change moves money or the delivery address, the page asks for your passkey. If the preview offers no **Apply the change** button, the change cannot be applied from this page.

To cancel the whole package with a full refund, use **Cancel the package** at the bottom of the page.
