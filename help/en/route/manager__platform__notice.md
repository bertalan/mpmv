# Notice
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This page shows one notice of illegal content and the next step of its workflow.

## What you see
Status, Received, what was Reported, Exact location, Explanation and Notifier (or Anonymous). Staff see the notifier; the vendor does not.

## The steps
1. Start review moves a received notice to under review.
2. Record the decision records the outcome and a reason.
3. When action is taken, Act on the content with Restrict and send the statement of reasons: the content is restricted and a statement is sent.
4. Suspected criminal offence (DSA article 18): Record the notification stores which authority was told and when. The platform does not send it for you.

Each step is only offered in the matching status. Recording a step may ask for your passkey. Questions on the rules: ask the compliance contact, {{ operator.compliance_email }}.
