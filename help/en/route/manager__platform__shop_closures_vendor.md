# Holidays and closures
last-checked: 2026-11-27

This page is for platform staff (Support and the platform owner). You manage the closures of the shop named at the top, and every change is recorded in the audit log with your name. A closure is a range of days when the shop does not dispatch orders, for example a holiday.

## What a closure is
Buyers can still order while the shop is closed. They see a neutral notice with the dispatch date. The notice has fixed wording and shows only dates. A change takes effect at once and needs no review.

## Add a closure
Fill in the form and press **Save the closure**.
- **First closed day** and **Last closed day**: both days are inclusive.
- **Repeats every year**: the same dates apply every year. 29 February cannot repeat.
- **Days of notice**: from 0 to 60, default 14. The notice appears this many days before the closure and stays until it ends.

## Past closures
A closure that has ended stays under **Past closures** as history. It cannot be edited or removed.
