# Payouts
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This page lists the money sent from the platform to your payout account, newest first.

## What you see
Each row shows the date, the amount sent (with a minus sign, because it lowers what the platform owes you), your balance after it and the payment provider's reference.

## What to do
Choose a period with **From** and **To** and press **Filter**. To check a payout in your bank account, look for the same amount and reference.
