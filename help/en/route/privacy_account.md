# Your data
last-checked: 2026-11-17

## What this page is for

Here you download a copy of your data or ask to erase your account.

## What you can do

- Press Download to get your data as a JSON file.
- To erase your account, tick the confirmation box and press Erase my account.
- Confirm with your password, or with your second factor if you have no password.

## Good to know

- Erasing blocks your login and deletes your carts and preferences. Some order, billing and tax data must be kept for the period written on the page, then it is anonymised.
- Read the text on the page before you erase: it states what is kept.
