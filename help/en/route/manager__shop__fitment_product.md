# Fits of a product
last-checked: 2026-11-19

Here you say what this product fits: specific items, everything, or nothing declared yet.

## What you can do
- Choose **Declaration**, then fill the rows: **Item**, **From year**, **To year**, **Excludes** and **Note**. Tick **Remove** to drop a row.
- To choose an **Item**, pick the make, then the model, then the variant in the lists that appear one after the other. Or type at least two letters in **Search by name** and choose a result with the mouse, or with the arrow keys and Enter. Escape closes the results. Without a script, a single list shows every approved item.
- Select **Preview** to see the changes. Nothing is written until you **Confirm**.
- Under **Copy from another product**, choose one of your products and select **Preview the copy**.
- If an item is missing, use **Propose a missing item**.

## Good to know
Only approved items can be chosen. A proposed item stays hidden until the platform reviews it. A shop can have at most 20 proposals waiting.
