# Fitment
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This page lists your products and what each one says it fits. Buyers who saved an item in their garage see only the products that fit it.

## What you can do
- Select a SKU to open the **Fits** page of that product.
- Tick products, choose **Add fitment to the selected products** under **Action on the selected rows**, then **Preview**. Nothing is written until you **Confirm**.
- Use **Search** and **Declaration** to narrow the list.
- **Fitment report** and **Propose a missing item** are in the links above the table.

## Good to know
A product that is not declared is hidden from a buyer with an active garage, in a category that needs fitment.
