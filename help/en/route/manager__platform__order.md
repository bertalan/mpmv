# Change an order
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This page changes one shop's package of a paid order. Platform staff may change the carrier to one at the same or a lower price, or correct the address when the customer asks. Products, quantities and prices belong to the shop.

## Steps
1. Fill in Prepare a change and choose Compute the change. Nothing is applied yet.
2. Read The change being prepared: what changes, the money refunded or to pay, and whether the change is minor or substantial, with the reasons.
3. Choose Apply the change (minor changes) or Discard the draft. When a change moves money or the delivery address, applying it asks for your passkey.

## Cancel the package
Cancel the package with a full refund refunds the customer in full. The cause you choose decides who pays the refund fee and whether the units go back to stock. It asks for your passkey.

## Good to know
Only a paid order that is waiting for the seller or accepted, and not shipped yet, can be changed. Only the side that started a draft may apply or discard it.
