# Your details
last-checked: 2026-11-17

## What this page is for

Here you give the delivery address and your e-mail address. It is the first step after the cart.

## What you can do

- Fill in e-mail, name, address and country.
- Tick I am buying for a business only if you buy as a company, then add the company name and VAT number.
- Press Continue to review.

## Good to know

- We use your details only to deliver the order and to issue the sellers' invoices.
- If a field shows an error, the summary at the top tells you which one to fix.
