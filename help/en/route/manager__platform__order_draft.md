# Prepared cart
last-checked: 2026-11-18

Use this page to check a cart prepared from a past order, change its quantities and send the customer the one-time link.

## Check the items
The table shows each item at today's catalog price, the units available and the quantity. Items that cannot be bought or delivered today are listed under Left out of the cart, with the reason.

You cannot change a price: the customer pays the catalog price of the moment they check out.

## Change the quantities
1. Change a quantity, up to the units available. Set it to 0 to remove the item.
2. Write the note and choose Save the quantities.

A change makes a link already sent stop working: send a new one.

## Send the link
1. Write the note and choose Send the link.

The link goes only to the email of the order's account, or to the guest's email for a guest order. It works once, expires after the days set by the operator (7 by default), and at most 3 links can be sent per day. A new link replaces the one sent before.

The customer opens the link, checks the cart and pays at checkout. You never place or pay the order for the customer. Every step is recorded with your role and note.
