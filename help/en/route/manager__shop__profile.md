# Storefront profile
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This page is for the owner of the shop. It holds the texts and policies customers read on your shop page, one language at a time, and your logo.

## How to edit
1. Under **Language of the texts**, choose the language to edit.
2. Change the fields, such as **Tagline**, **About the shop**, **Shipping policy**, **Returns and refunds policy** and **Cancellation policy**.
3. Press **Save**. The change is live at once, and the platform moderators are told.

Under **Logo**, choose a **Logo image**, describe it in **Description of the logo** and press **Save logo**. The description is for people who cannot see the image.

## Good to know
- A text marked as a machine draft is not public until you edit and save it.
- If your shop is suspended or closed, nothing can be changed here.
- Platform staff can revert a change.
