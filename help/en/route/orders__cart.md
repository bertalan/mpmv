# Your cart
last-checked: 2026-11-17

## What this page is for

This is your cart. Items are grouped by seller, because each seller is a separate sale.

## What you can do

- Change a quantity: the cart updates by itself, or press Update cart.
- Remove an item with Remove next to it.
- When everything is fine, press Continue to checkout.

## Good to know

- The price here is indicative. Shipping is worked out at checkout from your delivery address.
- A warning next to an item (for example not enough stock) must be solved before you can go on.
