# Statements of reasons
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This page lists the statements of reasons the platform has sent to your shop. A statement explains why the platform restricted or removed something of yours after a report. The page is read only: you cannot change a statement.

## What you see
- The newest statement is first.
- **Reference** is the number of the statement. **Notice reference** is the number of the report it follows.
- **What is restricted** says which offer, content or function is affected. **Valid until** gives the end date when there is one.
- **Facts and circumstances** and **Ground** explain the decision.
- A closing paragraph, when present, explains how you can appeal or ask for a review.

The statement does not name who sent the report.

If the page is empty, the platform has sent no statement for your shop. Questions about a statement: write to {{ operator.compliance_email }}.
