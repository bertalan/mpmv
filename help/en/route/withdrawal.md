# Withdrawal
last-checked: 2026-11-17

## What this page is for

Use this page to start a withdrawal from an order. It has two steps.

## What you can do

- Enter your order number and the e-mail address you used for the order.
- Complete the check on the page and press Continue.
- On the next page choose the sellers you withdraw from and confirm.

## Good to know

- Each seller in your order is a separate contract, so you choose per seller.
- Whether you can still withdraw is shown for each seller on the next page. For the rules, read the withdrawal information in the terms.
