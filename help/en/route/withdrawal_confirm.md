# Withdrawal received
last-checked: 2026-11-17

## What this page is for

This page appears after you confirm a withdrawal. It is the acknowledgement of what you sent.

## What you can do

- Read the order number, the date and the reference of each seller.
- Keep this page's reference and the e-mail we send as proof.

## Good to know

- You reach this page only by confirming on the previous page; do not reload it to send again.
- If the e-mail could not be queued, the reference on this page is your proof.
