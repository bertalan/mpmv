# Notice of termination
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This page shows one notice of termination given to a vendor.

## What you see
Status, Notice given, The service ends on, Notice period (days), the shorter-notice reason if any, Facts and circumstances and Ground (these are sent to the vendor), and the dates of withdrawal or application.

## What you can do
- While the notice is pending, staff who may write can use Withdraw the notice: enter a reason and choose Withdraw and tell the vendor.
- When the vendor is closed, Frozen orders and payouts shows how many open orders wait. Choose Record the review once checked: the payouts are released.

Both actions may ask for your passkey. Use Back to the terminations to return.
