# Apply to sell with us
last-checked: 2026-11-17

## What this page is for

This is the application form for a prospective seller. It asks only for your name, e-mail address and the name of your business.

## What you can do

- Fill in the form, complete the check and press Send my application.
- Open the e-mail we send you and confirm your address; you choose your password there.

## Good to know

- Our team reviews your application before you can sell.
- After you confirm your e-mail you sign in and set up an authenticator app, which every seller needs.
- An application nobody confirms is deleted after a few days.
