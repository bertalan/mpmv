# Set up two-factor authentication
last-checked: 2026-11-17

## What this page is for

On this page you choose how to confirm it is you after your password: an authenticator app or a passkey. If a factor is already set, it shows your protection.

## What you can do

- Choose Use an authenticator app, or add a passkey (the screen lock of this device or a security key).
- If you already have a factor, you can add another or open the two-factor settings.
- Open Sessions to see where you are signed in.

## Good to know

- Your role may require a second factor before you can continue.
- Passkeys need JavaScript and a browser that supports them.
