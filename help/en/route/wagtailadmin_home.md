# Dashboard
last-checked: 2026-11-17

This is the home screen of the editors' admin. The menu on the left holds the sections your role may open, such as pages, images, navigation and help texts.

## What you can do
- Read the summary panels and the list of your most recent edits.
- Open a page you edited lately to continue where you stopped.
- Use the menu to reach another section.

## Good to know
- The menu shows only what your role allows, so a colleague may see more or fewer items.
- Shop, order and payment tasks are not here: they are in the Manager.
