# Shop closures
last-checked: 2026-11-27

Choose a shop to manage its holidays and closures. This page is for platform staff (Support and the platform owner).

## Find a shop
Type part of the shop name in the search box and press **Search**, then open the shop from the list. Closed shops are not listed.

## What you can do
On the shop's page you can add, edit and remove closures, like the shop's own owner. A closure that has ended stays as history and cannot be changed, not even by staff.
