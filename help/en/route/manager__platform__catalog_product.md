# Product
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This page shows one product: its shop, status, category and the state of the text in each language.

## Fields staff may change
Staff with the Catalog and moderation role can change **Sales mode** and **Fiscal category**. Select **Preview** to see the change, then **Confirm** to save it. The change is recorded in the audit log. Prices, the owning shop and the texts are not changed here. Staff can also set **Shop role for this product**, which overrides the shop's choice for that one product; leave it on **Use the shop's default** to follow the shop.
