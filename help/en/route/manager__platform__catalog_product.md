# Product
last-checked: 2026-11-18

This page shows one product: its shop, status, category and the state of the text in each language.

The platform owner and the Support role see this page read-only, with a notice and without the change form.

## Fields staff may change
Staff with the Catalog and moderation role can change **Sales mode** and **Fiscal category**. Select **Preview** to see the change, then **Confirm** to save it. The change is recorded in the audit log. Prices, the owning shop and the texts are not changed here.
