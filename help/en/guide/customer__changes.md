# Changes and cancellations
last-checked: 2026-11-18

What you can change, before and after you pay.

## Before you pay

- On the review step you can go back and correct your details, or choose another shipping option.

## After you pay

- A seller may need to change an order, for example when an item is out of stock. Small changes can apply at once. Bigger ones are proposed to you and wait for your answer. You are told by e-mail and the order page shows it.
- If an item is not available, you may be offered to cancel the rest of that package with a refund, or to keep it. The offer stays open until the package ships.
- To correct your address or ask to cancel before the seller ships, write to {{ operator.support_email }} as soon as you can and tell us your order number. Once a package has shipped it can no longer be changed, but you may still have the right to withdraw (see Withdrawal and returns).
