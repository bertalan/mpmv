# Your account and privacy
last-checked: 2026-11-23

Where to find your orders and how your data is handled.

## Your orders

- [My orders]({{link.orders}}) lists your orders, newest first. Open one to see its details and status.
- If you ordered without an account, open the order from the link in your order e-mail.

## Your garage

- The menu at the top of the page shows your active vehicle. Open it and choose **Change vehicle**, or **Choose a vehicle** if you have none saved yet. With no garage the menu reads **Add your garage**.
- On the chooser, pick **Generic (show all)** to see everything, or one of your vehicles, then press **Apply**. **Cancel** takes you back and changes nothing.
- The active vehicle decides what the shop shows you as fitting. When you have vehicles saved but none applies, the menu says "generic".
- If you are signed in, **My garage** lets you rename, duplicate or delete vehicles.

## Your data

- On [your data page]({{link.privacy}}) you can download a copy of your data or ask to erase your account.
- Some order, billing and tax data must be kept for the period stated on that page. Read it before you erase.
- Cookie settings are linked in the footer.
- Questions: write to {{ operator.support_email }}.
