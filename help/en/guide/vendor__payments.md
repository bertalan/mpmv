# Payments
last-checked: 2026-11-18

How you get paid.

## The payout account
Only the owner of the shop sees the **Payments** page. Press **Connect your payout account** to start, or **Continue the onboarding** if you started before. You complete the steps with the payment provider and come back; the status is then updated. Do not stop halfway: the account stays inactive until everything asked is done.

## Selling mode
**Account status** tells you whether the account is active. **Selling mode** is **Online sale** or **On request**. Until the account is active, your products are shown in the catalog and are sold on request: customers cannot add them to the cart. **Still needed** lists what the provider still asks of you.
