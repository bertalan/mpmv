# Withdrawal and returns
last-checked: 2026-11-18

You may have the right to withdraw from a purchase. This page only shows you where to go. The rules themselves are written in the legal pages and we do not repeat them here.

## Where to find the rules

- Read the [consumer information]({{link.consumer_information}}): the right of withdrawal, how long it lasts and the legal guarantee.
- The terms of sale are linked in the footer of every page. The review step also shows, for each seller, how withdrawal and returns work for that seller.

## How to withdraw

- Use the [online withdrawal form]({{link.withdrawal}}). Enter your order number and the e-mail address you used, then choose the sellers you withdraw from. Each seller in your order is a separate contract.
- You receive an acknowledgement by e-mail.
