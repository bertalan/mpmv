# Changes after payment
last-checked: 2026-11-18

What to do when a paid order has to change.

## Change an order
You can change a paid order that is waiting for you or already accepted, as long as it has not shipped. Use **Change this order** on the order page.
1. Under **Prepare a change**, choose what to change and write the **Reason for the change**. The customer reads it, so use plain words.
2. Press **Compute the change**.
3. Check **What changes**, the totals and **Money**: whether the customer is refunded or has to pay.
4. Press **Apply the change**, or **Discard the draft**.

If the change moves money or the delivery address, the page asks for your passkey.

## Cancel the package
To cancel the whole package with a full refund, use **Cancel the package** at the bottom of the change page.

## Good to know
- A small change can apply at once. A bigger one is proposed to the customer and waits for the answer.
- After the package has shipped it can no longer be changed.
- If you changed an order after you issued an invoice, correct your document.
