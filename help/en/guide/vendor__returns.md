# Sales terms and returns
last-checked: 2026-11-18

The conditions your buyers read before they pay.

## Sales terms
- Under **Sales terms** (owners only) you see the version in force and **Versions**, the list of all published ones. Without a published version your products are sold on request only.
- To publish a new version, fill in the form (for example **Governing law**, **Delivery time in days**, **Who pays the return**) and press **Publish**. It goes into force at once.
- A published version is never edited. Orders already placed keep the version they were placed under.

## Shipping and return policies
In the storefront profile you can write the **Shipping policy**, the **Returns and refunds policy** and the **Cancellation policy**, one language at a time. Press **Save**; the change is live at once and the platform moderators are told.

This guide does not replace the legal texts. For the legal rules, read the platform terms. Questions: {{ operator.support_email }}.
