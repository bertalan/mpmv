# Security and notices
last-checked: 2026-11-18

Protect your account and read what the platform tells your shop.

## Two-factor authentication
After your password you confirm it is you with an authenticator app or a passkey. Keep at least one working factor and your recovery codes, so you cannot be locked out.

## Statements of reasons
**Statements of reasons** lists the statements the platform sent to your shop when it restricted or removed something after a report. The page is read only. Read **What is restricted**, **Facts and circumstances** and **Ground**. Questions: {{ operator.compliance_email }}.

## Termination notice
If the platform gives your shop a notice of termination, **Termination notice** shows **Notice given** and **The service ends on**. Until the end date you can keep selling. Press **Download my data** to get your shop profile, products and order amounts as a JSON file; it is available only while a notice is pending.
