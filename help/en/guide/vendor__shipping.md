# Shipping
last-checked: 2026-11-20

How shipping works for your shop.

## Methods and zones
Shipping methods, zones and rates are set by the platform team. There is no page in your shop area to change them yet. If a method or a country is missing for your goods, write to {{ operator.support_email }}.

## Shipping an order
1. Open the order in **Orders**. You see the shipping address while the order is open.
2. Pack the goods, then enter the **Tracking number** and the **Carrier**.
3. Press **Mark as shipped**.

After that the package can no longer be changed. See Orders for the other steps.
