# Shipping
last-checked: 2026-11-18

Who ships your order, how much it costs and how to follow it.

## How it works

- Each seller ships its own items, so one order can arrive in more than one parcel.
- Shipping is worked out for each seller from your delivery address. You choose the option in the review step, and the total updates.
- The review step also shows each seller's delivery time.

## Follow your order

- Open the order from [My orders]({{link.orders}}) to see the status steps of each parcel. If you ordered without an account, use the link in your order e-mail.
- When the seller has added a tracking link, you see Track the parcel on the order.
