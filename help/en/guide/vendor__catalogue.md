# Catalogue and images
last-checked: 2026-11-20

What you can see and set for your products.

## Your products
- The **Dashboard** counts your products under **Products**: **Published**, **Drafts**, **Retired** and **Out of stock**.
- You cannot create, edit or add images to a product in your shop area yet. Send changes to the team at {{ operator.support_email }}.

## Fitment
1. Open **Fitment** to see your products and which vehicles each one fits.
2. Open a product, change its rows, then press **Preview**. Nothing changes until you press **Confirm**.
3. To reuse a declaration, use **Copy from another product**, then **Preview the copy**.
4. If a vehicle or part is missing from the lists, use **Propose a missing item**. **Fitment report** shows how your declarations stand.

## Images
The only image you set yourself is your **Logo**, on the **Storefront profile** page (owners only).
