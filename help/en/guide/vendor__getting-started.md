# Getting started as a seller
last-checked: 2026-11-20

Apply, sign in and set up your shop.

## Apply and sign in
1. Fill in the application form: your name, your e-mail address and the name of your business.
2. Open the e-mail we send you and confirm your address. You choose your password there.
3. Our team reviews your application before you can sell.
4. Sign in and set up an authenticator app. Every seller needs a second factor.

## Your shop area
- The **Dashboard** shows the shop you work on, **Your role** (**Owner** or **Staff**) and the number of products: **Published**, **Drafts**, **Retired** and **Out of stock**.
- The menu shows only the pages your role may use. The storefront profile, the sales terms and the payments are for owners only.
- In the storefront profile you write the texts customers read on your shop page, one language at a time, and add your logo.
- Before you can sell we need your legal contact data: the business name, the address of the registered office, a phone number and an e-mail address. The phone number is required if you sell to consumers, because buyers see it in the withdrawal information and the order confirmation. Without it your shop cannot be activated or publish products.
- Connect your payout account before you expect to sell online (see Payments).
