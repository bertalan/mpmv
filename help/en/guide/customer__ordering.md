# Ordering
last-checked: 2026-11-18

Everything you need to place an order, step by step.

## How it works

1. Add items to your cart. Items are grouped by seller, because each seller is a separate sale.
2. Press Continue to checkout and give your delivery address and your e-mail address. You do not need an account to order.
3. Review the order: choose a shipping option for each seller and check the total.
4. Place the order, then pay in the next step.

## Good to know

- The price in the cart is indicative. Shipping is worked out at checkout from your delivery address.
- If you buy as a company, tick the business box and add the company name and VAT number.
- A warning next to an item (for example not enough stock) must be solved before you can go on.
- You get a confirmation by e-mail. Keep your order number.
