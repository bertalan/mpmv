# Orders
last-checked: 2026-11-18

How to handle the orders your shop receives.

## The list and one order
- **Orders** lists the orders of your shop. Use the status filter to narrow the list.
- Open an order to see what to send and where. You see the customer's name and, while the order is open, the shipping address. You do not see the customer's e-mail or billing data.

## Steps
1. **Accept the order** when it is new.
2. **Ship the order**: enter the **Tracking number** and the **Carrier**, then press **Mark as shipped**.
3. **Mark as delivered** once the goods have arrived.

Only the action allowed at the current **Status** is shown.

## Invoice
The platform does not issue your invoice or receipt. Issue your own, then press **I have issued the invoice or receipt** under **Invoice**.
