# Payment
last-checked: 2026-11-18

How you pay and what to do when something goes wrong.

## How it works

- You pay online, in the secure payment form on the page that opens after you place the order. The methods you can use are the ones shown in that form.
- Your payment details are entered in the form of our payment provider.
- After you pay, the page shows that the payment is being confirmed and updates by itself. Do not pay twice.
- Each seller issues its own invoice for its part of the order.

## If something goes wrong

- If the payment form does not load, reload the page after a moment.
- The items stay held for you only for a short time while the order is unpaid.
- If you are still stuck, write to {{ operator.support_email }} and tell us your order number.
