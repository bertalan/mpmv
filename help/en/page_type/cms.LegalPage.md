# Legal page
last-checked: 2026-11-17

A legal page holds one legal text, such as the terms, the privacy policy or the cookie policy, with the date it takes effect. Each published change is kept as a new version.

## Fields that matter
- Kind: which legal text this is. The site links to it where that text is needed.
- Effective date: the day the text starts to apply. It cannot be earlier than the date of the previous version.
- Body: write the text your legal adviser approved, using the sections block.
- Onerous clauses: only for the customer terms, a list of article numbers and titles that business buyers approve at checkout.

## Images and licences
This page type has no picture of its own. Pictures in a block come from the image library: each needs a licence from the allow list, an author and an alt text.

## Translations
Each language is its own page. Use the page actions to translate the page: the translation tool copies the source text and you translate it there, and each translation is published separately.

## Publishing and preview
Save draft keeps your changes private. Preview shows the page as visitors will see it. Publish puts it on the site. A language without its own published page has no such page: nothing shows the English text in its place. Check the effective date of each translation.
